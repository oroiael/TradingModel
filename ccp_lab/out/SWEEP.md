# Sensitivity — what the 5% target itself costs

Same rule, same data, same put. Only the premium the weekly call is written for changes. A lower target means a strike further out of the money, so less income but less of the upside capped away.

| premium target | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| 2% of underlying | -44.6% | -9.5% | -29.5% | -15.2% | +2.6% |
| 3% of underlying | -42.9% | -2.9% | -32.1% | -33.9% | -11.6% |
| 4% of underlying | -43.4% | -12.8% | -32.9% | -39.5% | -14.0% |
| 5% of underlying | -39.7% | -16.6% | -33.5% | -37.6% | -17.8% |

The 5% target is the most expensive setting tested in most years: it pins the strike closest to the money, which maximises both the premium and the frequency with which the stock is called away.
