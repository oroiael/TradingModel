**NYSE Arca**

##### Early Opening Auction

| 2:30 a.m. | Order Entry and Early Open Auction Imbalance Information Begins |
| :---- | :---- |
| 3:59 a.m. | Early Open Auction Imbalance Freeze begins |
| 4:00 a.m. | Early Open Auction Occurs (Limit Orders Only) |

##### Opening Auction

| 8:00 a.m. | Core Open Auction Imbalance Publication Begins |
| :---- | :---- |
| 9:29 a.m. | Requests to cancel MOO Orders and LOO Orders will be rejected. |
| 9:29:55 a.m. | Core Open Auction Imbalance Freeze begins: new MOO Orders and LOO Orders will be rejected |
| 9:30 a.m. | Core Open Auction occurs within Auction Collars |

##### Closing Auction

| 3:00 p.m. | Imbalance Publication Begins |
| :---- | :---- |
| 3:59 p.m. | Closing Freeze Period Begins; only offsetting MOC/LOC order accepted. Requests to cancel MOC/LOC orders will be rejected |
| 4:00 p.m. | Closing Auction occurs within Auction Collars |

#### Auction Imbalance Publication Information

|  | Description | NYSE | NYSE American NYSE Arca NYSE Texas |
| :---- | :---- | ----- | :---: |
| **Indicative Match Price** | Best price at which the maximum volume of shares, including the non-displayed quantity of Reserve Orders (Only during Imbalance Freeze), is tradable in the applicable auction, subject to the [**Auction Collars**](https://www.nyse.com/trade/auctions#auction-collars). |  | X |
| **Total Imbalance** | Total Imbalance quantity for all orders included in the imbalance calculations at the [**Reference Price.**](https://www.nyse.com/trade/auctions#reference-price) | X | X |
| **Market Imbalance** | The imbalance of any remaining MOO & Market Day (Core Auction and Trading Halt Auction) / Market Day & MOC (Closing Auction) orders that are not already matched at the [**Reference Price**](https://www.nyse.com/trade/auctions#reference-price). |  | X |
| **Paired Quantity** | Number of shares paired at the [**Reference Price**](https://www.nyse.com/trade/auctions#reference-price). | X | X |
| [**Auctions Collar**](https://www.nyse.com/trade/auctions#auction-collars) | Price collar thresholds for the Indicative Match Price for the Core Open Auction, Trading Halt Auction, or Closing Auction. |  | X |
| [**Reference Price**](https://www.nyse.com/trade/auctions#reference-price) | Price at which the imbalances are calculated. | X | X |
