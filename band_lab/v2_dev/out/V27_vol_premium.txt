========================================================================================
AND THEN THE HEDGING BILL
========================================================================================

  The numbers above are gross. Delta-hedging is not free: every hedge is a
  trade in the underlying, at the measured 6.70 bp round trip.

  A gamma position that is hedged H times a day pays roughly H x half a round
  trip in friction on the hedge legs alone, on the hedged notional. The
  variance you capture rises with H (you track the path more closely); so does
  the bill. The path length itself is the thing being chased and it is
  6,870 bp/day.

    hedge    1x/day  ->        3 bp/day of hedging friction  =       8% a year on hedged notional
    hedge    4x/day  ->       13 bp/day of hedging friction  =      34% a year on hedged notional
    hedge   13x/day  ->       44 bp/day of hedging friction  =     110% a year on hedged notional
    hedge   26x/day  ->       87 bp/day of hedging friction  =     219% a year on hedged notional
    hedge   78x/day  ->      261 bp/day of hedging friction  =     658% a year on hedged notional
    hedge  390x/day  ->     1306 bp/day of hedging friction  =    3292% a year on hedged notional

  Read that against the edge table. A vol edge of a few points a year on a 70%
  vol underlying is worth a few hundred bp a year. Hedging four times a day
  costs 34% a year. The friction is not a detail here; it is the
  whole trade.
